# Credits and licences: Strada autos concept

All car imagery on this concept is real photography from **Wikimedia Commons** (licences CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA only), fetched through the Commons API. No manufacturer press images, no Google Images, no 3D models. The earlier procedural 3D cars were removed.

Base photographs were cropped to 16:9, resized to 1600 px wide and converted to WebP (plus an 800 px copy). No other edits were made to them.

## AI-modified variants

Every image in `variants/` was produced by editing one of the base photographs above with Google Gemini image editing (model `gemini-3.1-flash-image-preview`), changing only the paint colour or the wheels and keeping framing, background and car shape. **Each variant is modified with AI from the original credited below, and is shared under the same licence as its original** (CC BY-SA 4.0 share-alike applies; the CC0 original needs no attribution but is credited anyway). The as-photographed colour of each car is the unmodified photograph.
